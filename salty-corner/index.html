Our story

A little shop on a sunny corner

The Salty Corner started with a simple idea: the best presents feel like a small shop window full of wonder. Books to read together, tunes that play when the lid lifts, dolls to hug, and a pinch of the Algarve in every box.

We choose each piece by hand, wrap it with care and send it on its way — so you can give something that feels thoughtful, every time.
